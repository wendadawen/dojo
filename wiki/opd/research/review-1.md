- C1 分布失配：GKD 摘要 "current KD methods for auto-regressive sequence models suffer from distribution mismatch between output sequences seen during training and those generated by the student during inference"；复合误差句出自 TML Introduction："if the student makes an early mistake that the teacher never makes, it finds itself diverging ever farther from the states it observed in training"。
- C2 λ 框架：GKD §3.1 "On-policy and supervised KD are instantiations of GKD with divergence set to forward KL and student data fractions λ to 1 and 0 respectively."；"we do not backpropagate through the student's sampling distribution"。
- C3/F2 per-token 散度：GKD 式 (2) "(1/L_y) ∑_{n=1}^{L_y} D(p_T(·|y_<n,x) ∥ p_S^θ(·|y_<n,x))"。

- C5/C6：TML "teaching a fixed number of bits per training episode regardless of the number of tokens used"；"the reverse KL is 'unhackable'..."；"We do not require a separate reward or labeling model either."；"Since it doesn't require a rollout to finish sampling to calculate the reward..."。
- C7 Qwen3 两阶段：§4.5 原文 off-policy（/think 与 /no_think 输出合并做 response distillation）与 on-policy（"The student model is then fine-tuned by aligning its logits with those of a teacher model (Qwen3-32B or Qwen3-235B-A22B) to minimize the KL divergence"）；确认原文未指明 KL 方向，页面正确保持同等粒度未补方向。
- C8/N1：Table 21 全部数字一致（AIME'24 55.0/67.6/74.4，pass@64 90.0/90.0/93.3，GPU 小时 17,920/1,800，AIME'25 42.8/55.5/65.5）；§4.7 "distillation achieves significantly better performance than reinforcement learning while requiring approximately only 1/10 of the GPU hours"；条件三条（Qwen3-8B、math and code-related queries、same off-policy distilled 8B checkpoint）均有原文。

- C10：TML "We should think of RL as spending most of its compute on search — rolling out a policy and assigning credit — rather than on making updates"；非零成功率脚注 "RL requires the base model to have nonzero success to start with"。
- C11/F1：GKD 式 (1) JSD 及 Huszár (2015) β→0 极限；"Our experiments indicate that optimal divergence seems to be task-dependent. - C13：TML "any practical learning rate that's greater than zero leads to a degradation of performance on the instruction-following evaluation"。
- C14：GKD 式 (5)；"as far as we know, we are the first to perform distillation and RL fine-tuning at the same time"；Related Work "it changes the regularization towards the initial policy to towards the teacher policy"；"no additional hyperparameters... leverage hyperparameter tuning insights from RLHF"。

- C17/N3：GKD "relative gains of 2.1× on summarization, 1.7× on machine translation, and 1.9× on arithmetic reasoning"；"on-policy GKD on the 5% subsampled dataset, without any ground-truth summaries, outperforms supervised KD and ImitKD with entire training dataset"；开销 "approximately 1.8×, 2× and 2.2×... student-teacher ratio of 38×, 12× and 3.8×"。
