**官方源码（huggingface/transformers commit 36deb0b5，src/transformers/models/qwen3_5_moe/modeling_qwen3_5_moe.py 等）**
- `Qwen3_5MoeRMSNormGated.__init__`：`self.activation = "silu"`（硬编码）；`Qwen3_5MoeGatedDeltaNet`：`conv1d = nn.Conv1d(..., bias=False, kernel_size=4, groups=conv_dim)`、`in_proj_b/a → num_v_heads`、`g = -self.A_log.float().exp() * F.softplus(a.float() + self.dt_bias)`、`query/key.repeat_interleave(num_v_heads//num_k_heads=4)`、`use_qk_l2norm_in_kernel=True`、`torch_recurrent_gated_delta_rule` 中 `delta=(v_t-kv_mem)*beta_t; state=state+k.unsqueeze(-1)*delta.unsqueeze(-2)`。
- `Qwen3_5MoeAttention`：`q_proj` 出宽 `num_attention_heads*head_dim*2`，`query_states, gate = torch.chunk(q_proj(x), 2, -1)`（无条件，不读 `attn_output_gate`），`attn_output = attn_output * torch.sigmoid(gate)` 在 `o_proj` 之前；`q_norm/k_norm = RMSNorm(head_dim=256)`。
