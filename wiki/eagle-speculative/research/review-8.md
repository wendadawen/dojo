## 来源核对依据（本轮核对到的版本与原文片段）

- **EAGLE-1**：Li et al. 2024, arXiv:2401.15077 **v3**。Introduction 原文首句核对到「Applying speculative sampling hinges on finding a draft model that mirrors the original LLM's functionality but with reduced latency」；「Despite the 7B model's potential as a draft model, its high overhead diminishes acceleration gains.」；「TinyLLaMA is trained on 3,000B tokens, whereas EAGLE is trained on 2-4B tokens.」；摘要「a latency speedup ratio of 2.7x-3.5x, doubled throughput, while maintaining the distribution of the generated text.」；Figure 1 说明为「Vicuna and LLaMA2-Chat inference latency on the MT-bench for greedy (temperature=0) settings.」。draft 结构核对到「adds only a lightweight plug-in (a single transformer decoder layer)」，「The Embedding layer and LM Head employ the parameters of the target LLM.」
- **EAGLE-3**：Li et al. 2025, arXiv:2503.01840 **v3**。§3.1 标题为「Inference Pipeline」；Figure 5 图注「Diagram of the EAGLE-3 inference pipeline, illustrating the three steps of the draft model.」；Figure 6 图注「Diagram of the attention causal masks during training-time test.」；§3.1 原文「not possible because the token "I" has not yet been checked by the target model」「we cannot obtain g_I」「Instead, we use the output a_I from the draft model in the previous step to replace g_I」；「We concatenate the k-dimensional vectors l, m, and h to form a 3k-dimensional vector」「pass it through a fully connected (FC) layer to reduce it to k-dimensions, obtaining a feature g」；「inputted into a single layer decoder, producing the output a」「we input a_I into the LM head and sample to obtain the draft token "do"」；§3.2「During training, we perform test steps, where we generate a and feed it back into the draft model for further training.」；§4 指标段「EAGLE-3 does not modify the target model's weights and uses strict speculative sampling acceptance conditions」；§4.1「As the input from the draft model itself increases, the acceptance rate of EAGLE drops significantly, whereas EAGLE-3's acceptance rate remains almost unchanged」；Table 1（Vicuna 13B / MT-bench / T=0）EAGLE=3.07x、EAGLE-2=4.26x、EAGLE-3=5.58x；摘要「a speedup ratio up to 6.5x, with about 1.4x improvement over EAGLE-2. In the SGLang framework, EAGLE-3 achieves a 1.38x throughput improvement at a batch size of 64.」；五个任务为 MT-bench / HumanEval / GSM8K / Alpaca / CNN-DailyMail。
- **K3 技术报告**：arXiv:2607.24653 **v2**（v1 2026-07-27，v2 2026-08-07），§4.1.4 Draft Model Fine-Tuning。核对到「fine-tune the pre-trained MTP layer into an EAGLE-3-style draft model, with the target model frozen and only the draft layer and its feature-fusion projection updated.」；「Since minimizing the conventional KL-divergence surrogate does not guarantee maximizing this rate for a capacity-limited draft model」与 Eq.(16) `L_LK = -log Σ_x min(p(x), q(x))`，「evaluated at temperature 1 and no auxiliary ground-truth cross-entropy term」；「taken from the outputs of the 1st, 4th, and final AttnRes blocks」「bias-free matrix W_E3, initialized as [0 0 I] so that the fused representation coincides at initialization with the high-level feature」；「MoE expert weights in MXFP4 and their input activations in MXFP8, while non-expert modules remain in higher precision」；「the draft is unrolled for seven steps during training」。摘要写全称「Attention Residuals」，与页内 §2.2 引注一致。
- **Leviathan et al. 2023**：arXiv:2211.17192 **v2**（2023-05-18）。Theorem 3.8 原文「The expected improvement factor in total walltime by Algorithm 1 is (1−α^{γ+1})/((1−α)(γc+1)).」；Corollary 3.6「α = 1 − E(DLK(p, q)) = E(min(p, q))」，且 `E_{x∼q} min(1, p(x)/q(x)) = Σ_x min(p(x),q(x))`；Appendix A.1 标题「Correctness of Speculative Sampling」，其内证明 `P(x = x0) = min(p,q) + p − min(p,q) = p(x0)`。
